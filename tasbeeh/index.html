Privacy Policy – Tasbeeh Counter

Developer: Haleem studio · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tasbeeh Counter Android app ("the app") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. You do not need an account to use it.

Your counts, saved dhikr and settings are stored only on your own device. We do not receive or upload this data, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID and basic device information (device model, operating system, language)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (for example, ads viewed or clicked) and diagnostic data

This data is collected and used by Google according to its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ads personalization at any time in your device: Settings → Google → Ads.

3. How information is used

  • To show ads that keep the app free
  • To prevent fraud and measure ad performance (by Google)

We do not sell personal information.

4. Data sharing

We do not share any data ourselves. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, as described above. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – needed to load ads
  • Vibrate – for haptic feedback when counting
  • Advertising ID – used by AdMob for ads

7. Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can control ads personalization from your device settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
